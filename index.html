about

Profile

I'm a self-taught blockchain developer specializing in Solidity smart contracts and Zero-Knowledge cryptography, with a focus on DeFi protocols, on-chain automation, and rigorous testing. I've built and deployed contracts integrating Chainlink VRF and Automation, and written ZK circuits in Noir — spanning Merkle Sum Tree proof-of-solvency and undercollateralized credit verification — with on-chain proof verification via Barretenberg's UltraHonk.

My foundation is in smart contract security: reentrancy, access control, and invariant & fuzz testing. I'm now working toward a Zero-Knowledge Engineer role as my primary goal, while staying open to smart-contract development roles at ZK-adjacent teams along the way.
